Our Service

Mirzam provides an elegant platform to craft bespoke digital invitations, share a personalized link with your guests, and seamlessly collect RSVPs and guestbook wishes.

Designing and previewing your suite is completely free and limitless. Publishing your invitation to the web requires selecting one of our premium plans or starting a complimentary 24-hour trial.

Plans and Hosting

Our current pricing is available on our Collections page, which serves as the official reference. Should our rates change, previously purchased plans remain completely unaffected.

  • Astra Plan: Your invitation link remains beautifully hosted and active for one full year following activation. Please note that this plan includes a delicate Mirzam watermark on your design.
  • Aurora Plan: Your invitation is hosted for a lifetime with no expiration date, and is completely watermark-free for a pure, uninterrupted aesthetic. This hosting is valid for as long as Mirzam continues to operate; in the unlikely event of service closure, we will provide ample notice and a complete archive of your data.
  • Complimentary Trial: Available once per design, this 24-hour preview allows you to view your invitation live. Please note that the trial does not include RSVP collection or guest wishes, and includes the Mirzam watermark.

Payment and Activation

We currently accept payments via manual transfer to a mobile wallet or InstaPay. Upon checkout, simply upload your transfer receipt and the sending number; our team will personally verify the transaction.

To ensure a seamless experience, your very first purchase will activate your invitation immediately upon uploading the receipt, prior to our manual review. Should we find any issues with the receipt, the link will be temporarily paused and we will reach out to you—rest assured, your design and data will remain safely stored.

All prices are listed in Egyptian Pounds (EGP). Please be aware that any bank or wallet transfer fees are the responsibility of the client.
