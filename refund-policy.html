Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Your trust means everything to us. This page outlines our binding 7-day money-back guarantee, ensuring your experience with Mirzam is completely risk-free.

7-day money-back guarantee

We want you to be completely in love with your Mirzam experience. If our service doesn't perfectly suit your needs, simply message us within 7 days of your payment for a full refund.

To ensure fairness, we only require that your refund request is made at least 14 days before your scheduled event date. Other than that, there are absolutely no hidden clauses—we will not ask for a reason, and your refund is guaranteed even if you have already published your invitation.

How to request a refund

To request a refund, simply send us a direct message on Instagram with the link to your invitation.

Once approved, your refund will be transferred back to your original payment method (InstaPay or mobile wallet) within three business days.

Kindly note: Any standard transaction fees applied by your bank or wallet provider are beyond our control.

Beyond the Guarantee Period

While we cannot offer standard refunds after the initial 7-day window, your peace of mind remains our priority.

If an unexpected technical issue prevents you from sharing your invitation, please reach out to us. We will dedicate ourselves to resolving the problem immediately, or issue a full refund if we cannot.

This is our genuine promise to you, crafted with the same care as your invitation.
