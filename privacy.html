Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Your privacy matters to us. Here is a clear guide on what information we collect, who can see it, and how you can delete it at any time.

Who we are

Mirzam is a service for creating premium digital wedding and engagement invitations. This page explains what information we keep, why we need it, and how you can control it.

For any questions please message us on Instagram.

Information you share with us

  • Account details: Your name, and your email or phone number. Your password is fully encrypted, meaning neither we nor anyone else can ever see it.
  • Invitation content: All the beautiful details you add to your card—like your names, the event date and location, photos, personal messages, and background music.
  • Contact details: We handle most of our communication through Instagram, but you can provide an optional phone number if you prefer us to reach you that way. We will never use this number for anything else.
  • Payment proof: A screenshot of your transfer receipt and your InstaPay handle or wallet number so we can confirm your payment.
  • Automatic security data: When you use our site or create an account, our systems automatically note basic technical details like your device type and IP address (your digital location). We strictly use this to keep your account safe from hackers and to keep you logged in.

Your guests' data

When your guests open your invitation, they may submit their name, their RSVP status (including the number of accompanying guests), and a written message for your guestbook.

This data belongs to you. We do not sell it, share it with any one, or use it for marketing. However, we may occasionally reach out to ask for your permission to feature your invitation as a live example for future customers.

You have full admin powers to moderate your guestbook, meaning you can easily delete or manage any guest wishes that appear on your invitation.

Third-party services on the site

To keep the site running smoothly and securely, we use the following trusted services:

  • Firebase (by Google): We use this to securely store your invitation data (like RSVPs and guestbook wishes), manage user accounts, and track basic website visit statistics.
  • Cloudinary: We use this to securely host, store, and deliver the photos and voice messages uploaded by you and your guests.
  • GitHub: We use this to host the website files online.

These services operate under their own strict privacy and security policies.

How long we keep data

  • Astra plan: Your invitation link stays active online for one full year from activation.
  • Aurora plan: Your invitation link remains live forever with lifetime hosting.
  • Free trial: The preview link expires after 24 hours, but your saved design stays in your account.

Payment receipts are kept securely for accounting and verification records.

You can ask us to permanently remove your account and all associated details at any time—just send us a message on Instagram.

Your data rights

You have the right to request a copy of your data, correct any inaccuracies or delete your account entirely.

To make any of these requests, simply message us on Instagram and we will assist you promptly.

Data security

We take your privacy seriously and use trusted, modern security measures to protect your account and data. Access to your information is strictly limited to our team when you need support.

While no digital system is 100% perfect, we are committed to keeping your memories safe and will always notify you if a security issue ever occurs.
